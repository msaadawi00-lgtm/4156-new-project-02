Who We Are

Welcome to Weimar Travel Agency,we are your local guide to the secrets, stories, and hidden gems of this UNESCO World Heritage city. From grand ducal palaces and world-class museums to cozy cafes tucked into historic alleys, we help you experience Weimar like a local.

Weimar is more than just a destination on a map; it is a living canvas where classical philosophy, revolutionary literature, and modernist design intersect. From the footsteps of Goethe and Schiller to the avant-garde vision of the Bauhaus movement, our mission is to open the door to Weimar's rich stories and help you experience them in a way that is personal, meaningful, and unforgettable.
