Our Services | Crafting Your Unforgettable Weimar Experience

Elevate Your Journey to Germany's Cultural Heart

At Weimar Travel Agency, we believe traveling should be an inspiring discovery, not a logistical puzzle. Whether you are looking to dive deep into Weimar's rich literary history, explore its modernist Bauhaus roots, or enjoy a relaxing escape among royal palaces and sprawling parks, we offer comprehensive, bespoke travel services tailored to your every wish.

Explore our core services below to see how we bring your dream Weimar trip to life

1. Custom Itinerary Design & Travel Planning

Your trip, built entirely around your personal vision.

We do not believe in standard, mass-market travel packages. Our flagship itinerary design service begins with an in-depth consultation to understand your unique interests, preferred travel style, mobility needs, and budget.

• What's Included:

  • Fully personalized, day-by-day itineraries tailored to your pace.
  • Curated lists of must-see landmarks, hidden courtyards, and local secrets.
  • Handpicked dining recommendations and confirmed reservations at top local restaurants.
  • Smart daily routing to optimize walking times and avoid crowded hours.
  • Best For: Solo travelers, couples, and families seeking a stress-free, customized adventure.

2. Private Guided Cultural & Historical Tours

Uncover Weimar's stories alongside expert local historians.

Step beyond standard guidebooks. Led by passionate local guides, historians, and art experts, our private walking and driving tours bring Weimar's legendary eras to life with rich storytelling.

• Featured Tour Themes:

  • The Classical Golden Age: Walk in the footsteps of Johann Wolfgang von Goethe and Friedrich Schiller, visiting their historic homes and the iconic Duchess Anna Amalia Library.
  • The Bauhaus & Modernist Movement: Explore the birthplace of 20th-century design with guided visits to the Bauhaus Museum, Van de Velde Building, and Haus am Horn.
  • Ducal Palaces & Romantic Parks: Enjoy a relaxing, picturesque stroll through the Park on the Ilm, the Roman House, and the grand estate of Schloss Belvedere.
  • Best For: History enthusiasts, art lovers, and travelers who appreciate deep, authentic local insights.

3. Curated Multi-Day Travel Packages

Seamlessly bundled escapes for an effortless getaway.

For travelers who prefer a ready-to-book, multi-day experience, we offer carefully designed packages structured around specific themes and seasons.

• Popular Curated Escapes:

  • The Weimar Classic Weekend (3 Days / 2 Nights): A curated highlights journey featuring guided literary tours, fine dining, and boutique accommodation.
  • Art, Design & Architecture Immersion (4 Days / 3 Nights): A deep dive into Weimar's evolution from ducal Rococo grand elegance to functional Bauhaus design.
  • Seasonal Nature & Culture Retreat: Scenic parkland walks, cozy traditional cafe visits, and evening concert performances.
  • What's Included: Premium hotel stays, advance museum ticket reservations, private tours, and custom digital travel guides.

Complete peace of mind from arrival to departure.

Travel should be a true escape. Let us handle the friction points behind the scenes so you can focus entirely on enjoying your time in Weimar.
