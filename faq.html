Booking & Payments

  1. Do you charge planning or service fees?

    2. No, our core vacation planning and itinerary design services are completely free of charge. We earn our commission directly from the resorts, cruise lines, and tour operators we partner with, meaning you get expert advice at no extra cost.

  2. Do I have to pay for my trip upfront in full?

    3. No, you do not. Most bookings only require an initial deposit to secure your spot. The remaining balance is typically due a set number of weeks or months before your departure date, depending on the specific tour operator or resort policy.

  3. you match or beat prices I find online?

    4. Yes, we often can. Through our exclusive industry partnerships and wholesale supplier networks, we have access to special rates, package deals, and added perks that aren't available to the general public booking on standard consumer websites.

Support, Safety & Insurance

  1. What happens if my flight is delayed or my trip is canceled?

    2. We provide 24/7 emergency support while you are traveling. If your flight is delayed or canceled, call us immediately. We will handle the rebooking, coordinate with airlines or hotels on your behalf, and help you navigate your travel insurance claims.

  2. Is travel insurance included or required?

    3. Travel insurance is not automatically included in your package, but it is highly recommended. We offer comprehensive insurance plans through trusted providers during the booking process to protect your investment against unexpected medical emergencies, trip interruptions, or cancellations.

Planning & Customization

  1. What travel documents do I need to bring?

    2. At a minimum, international travel requires a passport that is valid for at least six months beyond your return date. Depending on your destination, you may also need visas, proof of vaccinations, or digital entry forms. We will provide a customized checklist for your specific trip.

  2. Can I customize a pre-made tour package?

    3. Yes, many of our pre-made tour packages are fully customizable. We can adjust accommodations, activities, and transportation options to better suit your preferences, budget, and travel style. Just let us know what you want to change, and we will work with our partners to create a tailored experience.
